## CRITICAL DISTINCTIONS — READ CAREFULLY

### Chess Rank vs Game Level
Players have a **chess rank badge** showing their account tier: Pawn, Knight, Bishop, Rook, Queen, King.
This badge often shows "Lv XX" beneath it (e.g. "Bishop-1 / Lv 20") — this is the **chess ranking XP level**, NOT the in-game board level.
The **in-game board level** (1–10) is shown at the **bottom of the screen** near the XP progress bar (small number, usually 8–11). Report ONLY the in-game board level. Never report the chess rank "Lv XX" as the game level.

### Chess Rank names are NOT synergies and NOT talents
"Bishop", "Rook", "Queen", "King", "Knight", "Pawn" followed by a dash and number (e.g. "Bishop-1", "Knight-4") are account-tier rank badges. Do NOT list them as synergies or talents. They are irrelevant to the game result.

### Chat messages are NOT relics or game data
Ignore all text in the in-game chat panel.

### How to read synergies
Active synergy counts are shown as a row of small badges, each with a synergy icon and a number: e.g. "(4) (2) (2) (1)". This means there are 4 active synergies with those counts. Identify each synergy by its icon.
**Read those badge numbers directly — do NOT count unit models on the board.** Board unit counts do not equal synergy counts.
Output ALL synergy badges you can read, not just the top one or two.

### Synergy output format
Use PIPE "|" as separator. Use EXACT names from the Known Synergies list (e.g. "Troll" not "Trolls", "Knight" not "Knights").
Example: Knight:4|Troll:2|Demon:2|Warlock:1

### Talents
Talents are in-game passive bonuses chosen from a talent selection menu during the match. They appear in a "Tal" column in the final scoreboard (dac3-style). They are NOT chess rank names. If no talent column or talent info is visible, output "".
